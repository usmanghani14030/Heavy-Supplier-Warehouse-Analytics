
============================================================
WEEK-03 — INVENTORY RISK & DEMAND EXPLORATION
============================================================

PROJECT:
Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

SPRINT OBJECTIVE:
Analyse inventory risk, product demand trends and stock
movement activity to identify products requiring management
attention and support better inventory planning decisions.


1. OVERSTOCK & UNDERSTOCK DETECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

The inventory analysis covered 180 product-branch records
across 30 products and 6 branches.

All 180 records were classified as Overstock based on the
current stock being above the defined maximum stock level.

The result indicates a significant inventory-control issue
within the supplied dataset, with stock levels consistently
above the configured maximum thresholds.

The highest inventory-value exposure was concentrated in
high-value products such as:

- P015 — Engine Block EB-600
- P014 — Transmission Assembly TA-800
- P021 — Boom Cylinder BC-400
- P004 — Track Chain TC-45
- P019 — Swing Motor SWM-350

These products should receive priority attention when
reviewing inventory levels and capital tied up in stock.


2. PRODUCT DEMAND TREND ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Demand trends were analysed across 72 months for all
30 products.

Demand trend classification produced:

- 10 Decreasing
- 7 Strongly Decreasing
- 6 Strongly Increasing
- 3 Increasing
- 4 Stable

Therefore, 17 products showed declining demand while
9 products showed increasing demand.

The strongest increasing-demand product was:

P001 — Hydraulic Pump HP-300
Demand change: +39.96%

Other strongly increasing products included:

- P002 — Engine Oil Filter OF-90
- P008 — Bucket Tooth BT-10
- P013 — Seal Kit SK-15
- P026 — Grease Cartridge GC-5
- P027 — O-Ring Set OR-50

The strongest declining-demand product was:

P023 — Idler Wheel IW-55
Demand change: -46.81%

Other strongly declining products included:

- P010 — Turbocharger TB-900
- P022 — Control Valve CV-75
- P018 — Wheel Rim WR-24
- P017 — Fan Belt FB-30
- P016 — Radiator RD-250
- P021 — Boom Cylinder BC-400


3. MOVEMENT-BASED INVENTORY AGING
------------------------------------------------------------

Inventory aging was assessed using stock-ledger movement
activity because the inventory master does not contain a
direct stock acquisition or stock-age date.

The latest recorded stock movement date was:

28 January 2025

All 180 product-branch combinations had a stock movement
within the previous 30 days of the latest recorded movement.

Therefore:

- 180 records were classified as 0-30 Days
- 180 records were classified as Normal
- No movement-based aging risk was identified

This should not be interpreted as proof that the physical
stock is newly purchased. It indicates that the products
had recent recorded stock movement in the supplied ledger.


4. INVENTORY + DEMAND CROSS-ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Inventory position was combined with demand trends to
identify higher-priority inventory risks.

The 30 products were classified as:

- 7 High Risk — Overstock + Strong Demand Decline
- 11 Medium Risk — Overstock + Demand Decline
- 6 Growth Opportunity — Overstock + Rising Demand
- 6 Monitor — Overstock + Stable/Moderate Demand

The seven high-risk products were:

- P021 — Boom Cylinder BC-400
- P010 — Turbocharger TB-900
- P022 — Control Valve CV-75
- P023 — Idler Wheel IW-55
- P018 — Wheel Rim WR-24
- P016 — Radiator RD-250
- P017 — Fan Belt FB-30

These products combine excess inventory with strongly
declining demand and therefore represent the highest
priority for inventory review.


5. BUSINESS INSIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The combined analysis highlights a potential mismatch
between inventory levels and demand.

The dataset shows widespread overstocking while a large
proportion of products have declining demand. This can
increase working capital requirements and create a risk
of inefficient inventory allocation.

At the same time, several products have rising demand.
These products should be monitored separately because
their higher inventory levels may be more commercially
justifiable.

The analysis therefore supports a differentiated inventory
strategy rather than applying the same action to every
product.


6. RECOMMENDED MANAGEMENT FOCUS
------------------------------------------------------------

Based on the analysis, management should prioritise:

1. Reviewing maximum-stock parameters for all branches.
2. Investigating the seven high-risk products with declining
   demand and excess stock.
3. Monitoring products with increasing demand separately.
4. Reviewing demand trends before future replenishment
   decisions.
5. Investigating why current stock levels consistently exceed
   configured maximum levels.
6. Combining future forecasting with inventory thresholds
   before making procurement decisions.
