# Week 02 — Product & Inventory Analytics

## Overview
This week focuses on product performance, inventory efficiency, stock health, profitability, and inventory value concentration.

## Analyses Completed

1. Product Performance Analysis
2. Fast-Moving Product Identification
3. Slow-Moving Product Identification
4. Inventory Turnover Analysis
5. Stock Health Analysis
6. ABC / Pareto Inventory Analysis
7. Product Profitability Analysis
8. Product Performance Master Summary

## Key Findings

- 30 products were analysed.
- P015 — Engine Block EB-600 ranked #1 by sales value.
- P015 also ranked #1 by estimated gross profit.
- P029 — Pressure Sensor PS-90 ranked #1 by quantity sold.
- 9 products were classified as A-class products.
- 7 products were classified as B-class products.
- 14 products were classified as C-class products.
- P015 represented 21.48% of total inventory value.
- The top 9 A-class products represented approximately 79.64% of inventory value.
- All 180 analysed inventory records were above their maximum stock level.
- No analysed records were below safety stock.

## Inventory Turnover Note

Inventory turnover was calculated as COGS divided by current inventory value. Because opening/average inventory data was not available, this should be interpreted as a current-inventory turnover proxy rather than a formal annual inventory turnover ratio.

## Business Implications

- High-value A-class products require close inventory monitoring.
- Overstocking creates potential working-capital exposure.
- Fast-moving products should be monitored to maintain availability.
- Slow-moving products should be reviewed to reduce excess inventory.
- High-value and high-profit products should receive priority in inventory planning.
