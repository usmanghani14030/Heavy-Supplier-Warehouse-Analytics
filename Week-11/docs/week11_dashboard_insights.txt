7. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Prioritise high-risk product-branch combinations for physical
   inventory verification.

2. Investigate stock shrinkage through cycle counts and warehouse
   reconciliation.

3. Review unusual movement-frequency patterns for potential process
   errors or control weaknesses.

4. Strengthen inventory transaction controls for adjustments and
   stock corrections.

5. Monitor inventory levels where current stock exceeds defined
   maximum-stock thresholds.

6. Maintain validation rules for purchase-order status and
   received-date consistency.

7. Use the Week-11 dashboard as an operational monitoring layer for
   recurring risk identification.
