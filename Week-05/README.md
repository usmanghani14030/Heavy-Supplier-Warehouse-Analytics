# Week 5 — Core Product, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

## Project
Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

## Sprint Focus
Core Product, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

## Analyses Completed

### 1. Stock Health Analysis
Analysed product inventory health and identified Healthy, Watch and At Risk products.

### 2. ABC / Pareto Product Classification
Classified 30 products into A, B and C categories based on revenue contribution.

Key finding:
- A-class: 9 products
- B-class: 7 products
- C-class: 14 products
- A-class products contribute 78.49% of total revenue.

### 3. Product Demand Trend Analysis
Analysed 72 months of product demand from January 2019 to December 2024.

Key findings:
- Peak demand: July 2020 — 22,304 units
- Lowest demand: February 2019 — 14,117 units

### 4. Product Priority Analysis
Combined ABC classification, stock movement and stock health to identify products requiring attention.

Key findings:
- Critical: 4 products
- High: 2 products
- Medium: 16 products
- Low: 8 products

### 5. Warehouse Space Utilisation
Compared inventory stock density and warehouse space utilisation across six warehouses.

### 6. Warehouse Throughput
Measured warehouse orders, units handled and throughput efficiency.

Highest throughput efficiency:
- Kolkata East Depot — 8.57 units/sqft

Lowest throughput efficiency:
- Delhi Central — 4.53 units/sqft

### 7. Warehouse Capacity Analysis
Assessed warehouse capacity pressure using current inventory and warehouse capacity.

Key finding:
- Kolkata East Depot has the highest capacity pressure at 120.49%.

### 8. Overall Inventory & Warehouse Risk
Combined product and warehouse analysis to identify priority operational risks.

Critical products:
- P014 — Transmission Assembly TA-800
- P023 — Idler Wheel IW-55
- P018 — Wheel Rim WR-24
- P024 — Track Roller TR-60

## Final Insights

The analysis shows that a small group of A-class products generates a large proportion of revenue, while several products require inventory attention because of At Risk or Dead Stock conditions.

Warehouse analysis highlights Kolkata East Depot as the main capacity-pressure concern, while Delhi Central has the lowest throughput efficiency.

These findings can support inventory prioritisation, warehouse capacity planning and operational decision-making.
