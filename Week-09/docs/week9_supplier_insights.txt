OVERALL BUSINESS INSIGHTS
-------------------------
1. SUP0006 is the strongest overall-performing supplier and represents
   a strong candidate for long-term strategic collaboration.

2. SUP0005 has high strategic importance and the highest sourcing
   priority, but its longer lead time, procurement risk exposure, and
   cost position require active management.

3. SUP0001 presents a significant cost-management opportunity because
   it has the highest average PO value and the highest average
   purchase cost.

4. Procurement spend is relatively distributed across suppliers, with
   the top three suppliers accounting for 38.10% of total spend.
   However, the overall dependency score remains high and should be
   monitored.

5. Supplier quality and procurement risk require continued monitoring,
   particularly for suppliers with weaker overall scorecard results.

6. Supplier-product analysis should be used to identify opportunities
   for supplier diversification and product-level cost benchmarking.

7. Negotiation efforts should focus on suppliers with relatively high
   procurement costs and meaningful spend exposure.

8. Strategic sourcing should balance supplier performance, cost,
   quality, delivery reliability, and dependency risk rather than
   relying on procurement spend alone.

RECOMMENDED PROCUREMENT ACTIONS
-------------------------------
- Maintain SUP0006 as a strategic supplier and explore long-term
  partnership opportunities.
- Closely monitor SUP0005 because of its high strategic importance,
  risk exposure, and long lead time.
- Benchmark SUP0001 purchasing costs and negotiate improved pricing.
- Develop diversification plans for suppliers identified with high
  diversification priority.
- Continue supplier quality monitoring and periodic risk scoring.
- Use supplier-product cost variance analysis for procurement
  negotiations.
- Review supplier dependency regularly to reduce disruption risk.
- Use the overall supplier scorecard as a recurring supplier
  performance-management framework.
