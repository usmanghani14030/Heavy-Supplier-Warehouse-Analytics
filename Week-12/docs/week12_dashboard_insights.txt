WEEK 12 - EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD INSIGHTS
======================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The final Week-12 dashboard consolidates product, inventory, supplier, customer, operational risk, stock shrinkage and data integrity information for management-level review.
Total product sales revenue analysed was 22,958,987,430.00, with 1,234,939 units sold across 30 products.

2. PRODUCT PERFORMANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Engine Block EB-600 generated the highest product revenue at 4,856,985,000.00, representing approximately 21.16% of analysed product revenue.
The analysis covers 30 products, providing a complete view of product-level sales performance.

3. INVENTORY PERFORMANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
8 products were classified as fast-moving.
3 products were classified as slow-moving and 5 products as dead stock.
Therefore, 8 products require inventory management attention because they fall into slow/dead stock categories.

4. SUPPLIER PERFORMANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
8 suppliers were evaluated with an average supplier score of 57.14.
The highest-ranked supplier in the dashboard is SUP0006 with a score of 78.36.
Supplier performance should continue to be monitored using delivery reliability, quality, cost efficiency and procurement risk indicators.

5. CUSTOMER PERFORMANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard analyses 500 customers.
The largest customer segment is Regular Customers, containing 141 customers.
Customer segmentation can be used to prioritize retention, service differentiation and targeted commercial activity.

6. OPERATIONAL RISK
----------------------------------------------------------------------
180 product-branch combinations were assessed for operational risk.
180 combinations were classified as High Risk.
The average operational risk score was 99.87.
The highest-risk combination identified was P025 / HYD001.
Management attention should focus on high-risk product-branch combinations and the underlying shrinkage and movement-anomaly drivers.

7. STOCK SHRINKAGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
180 product-branch records showed negative stock variance.
Total absolute stock variance was 69,656 units.
The largest negative variance was associated with P025 / HYD001.
Stock reconciliation, movement monitoring and branch-level inventory controls should be prioritised for shrinkage-heavy locations.

8. DATA INTEGRITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
2 integrity issue categories were documented in the final dashboard data.
The identified issues were preserved and documented rather than silently corrected or overwritten.

9. WAREHOUSE UTILISATION DATA QUALITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Warehouse utilisation was deliberately excluded from validated management KPIs because the source calculations produced values above the valid 0-100% percentage range.
No artificial scaling or adjustment was applied. The original warehouse data remains preserved for future source-level review.

10. STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT PRIORITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Priority 1: Investigate high-risk product-branch combinations and recurring stock shrinkage.
Priority 2: Reduce slow-moving and dead-stock exposure through inventory rationalisation and demand-aligned replenishment.
Priority 3: Continue supplier performance monitoring with emphasis on reliability, quality, cost and procurement risk.
Priority 4: Use customer segmentation to improve retention and targeted commercial decisions.
Priority 5: Strengthen data-quality controls around inventory hierarchy and purchase-order completion fields.
