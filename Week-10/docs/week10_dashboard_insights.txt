2. PRODUCT & INVENTORY INSIGHTS

Product revenue is concentrated among a small number of high-value products.

Top revenue-generating products include:
- P015 Engine Block EB-600
- P014 Transmission Assembly TA-800

- P019 Swing Motor SWM-350
- P004 Track Chain TC-45

Eight products were classified as fast-moving based on unit sales.
Eight products were classified as slow/dead stock in the final dashboard
classification.

Management should prioritize high-revenue and fast-moving products while
reviewing slow/dead stock for inventory optimisation opportunities.


3. WAREHOUSE INSIGHTS
---------------------
Six warehouses were analysed with a combined stated capacity of
226,080 sqft.

The warehouse utilisation percentage field was identified as invalid
because the underlying occupied-footprint values were inconsistent with
the stated warehouse capacity.

Therefore, the invalid utilisation KPI was deliberately excluded from
the final KPI framework rather than presenting misleading information.

Warehouse capacity remains included as a validated KPI.

This data-quality issue should be investigated before using utilisation
percentages for operational decisions.

7. DATA QUALITY NOTES
---------------------
The warehouse utilisation KPI was excluded because its calculated values
were outside a valid percentage range.

This is an intentional data-quality control and prevents invalid metrics
from being presented in the executive dashboard.

The final KPI framework contains 14 validated KPIs across four categories.


8. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------
- Prioritize high-revenue products for availability and replenishment.
- Monitor fast-moving products closely to reduce stockout risk.
- Review slow/dead stock for potential inventory reduction.
- Use supplier performance and risk scores in sourcing decisions.
- Diversify suppliers where dependency risk is elevated.
- Apply targeted customer strategies by industry segment.
- Use customer lifetime value to prioritize high-value accounts.
- Investigate warehouse source data before calculating utilisation-based
  performance targets.
- Maintain a centralized KPI and dashboard layer for management reporting.


9. WEEK-10 CONCLUSION
---------------------
Week 10 established the project's Business Intelligence and KPI layer.
Validated operational metrics were consolidated into an executive KPI
framework, dashboard datasets were prepared, and seven dashboard

The dashboard layer provides a foundation for the final Weeks 11-12
strategy, advanced analytics, anomaly/risk monitoring, and final delivery.
