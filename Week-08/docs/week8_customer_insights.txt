8. OVERALL CUSTOMER ANALYTICS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The combined Week 8 analysis indicates that the customer base has
strong historical engagement, substantial repeat purchasing and a
large high-value customer population.

Key findings:
- South is the leading region by total revenue and customer count.
- North has the highest average customer revenue and order frequency.
- Customer C0153 is the strongest customer across multiple metrics,
  including revenue, frequency and CLV.
- 250 customers are classified as High Value or Very High Value.
- 68 customers are classified as Champions under RFM segmentation.
- 115 customers require retention action under the retention analysis.
- 192 customers require action under the more conservative churn
  analysis.
- Revenue at risk from churn-related customers is
  10,991,751,385.20.
- All 500 customers are repeat customers in the analysed delivered
  order history.
- 128 customers have High or Very High purchase frequency.
- Central and North East require additional attention because of
  comparatively low regional performance scores.

BUSINESS PRIORITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Protect Champions and Very High Value customers through proactive
   retention and relationship management.

2. Prioritise the 192 customers identified by churn analysis for
   targeted retention and reactivation activities.

3. Develop re-engagement campaigns for the 218 recently inactive
   customers identified in retention analysis.

4. Focus regional growth initiatives on Central and North East while
   maintaining the strong performance of South and North.

5. Use customer value, RFM score, purchase frequency and churn risk
   together to prioritise customer-management actions.

6. Monitor high-value customers with declining purchase activity so
   that revenue loss can be addressed before customers become fully
   churned.
