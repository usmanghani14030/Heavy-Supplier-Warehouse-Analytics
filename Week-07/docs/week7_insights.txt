WEEK 7 - SUPPLIER, PROCUREMENT & SOURCING ANALYTICS
====================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Week 7 focused on evaluating supplier cost performance, procurement
efficiency, supplier dependency, procurement risk, delivery risk,
sourcing diversification, and overall supplier performance.

DATASET
-------
Suppliers analysed: 8
Purchase orders analysed: 24,000
Received purchase orders: 21,630
Cancelled purchase orders: 2,370

KEY FINDINGS
------------

1. SUPPLIER COST PERFORMANCE
Supplier purchase costs were analysed using weighted unit costs and
cost variance metrics.

Weighted cost variance ranged from approximately 5.92% to 6.32%.

The highest weighted cost variance was observed for Guangzhou Local
Vendor Supplies at 6.32%, while Hangzhou OEM Supplies had the lowest
at 5.92%.

2. SUPPLIER CONCENTRATION / DEPENDENCY
Supplier purchasing was relatively diversified across all 8 suppliers.

Total purchase value:
346,621,977,539.14

Top supplier purchase share:
12.91%

Top 3 supplier purchase share:
38.10%

Top 5 supplier purchase share:
63.22%

All suppliers were classified as Moderate Dependency and the overall
concentration assessment was Diversified.

This indicates that procurement spending is not heavily dependent on
a single supplier, although supplier concentration should continue
to be monitored.

3. PROCUREMENT EFFICIENCY
Average procurement efficiency score:
90.12

Highest score:
90.59

Lowest score:
89.72

Five suppliers were classified as Excellent Procurement Efficiency
and three as Good Procurement Efficiency.

Qingdao Distributor Supplies achieved the highest procurement
efficiency score of 90.59%.

The main improvement area for lower-ranked suppliers is reducing
purchase order cancellations.

4. SUPPLIER PROCUREMENT RISK
Average procurement risk score:
69.50

Highest risk score:
84.46

Lowest risk score:
54.93

Four suppliers were classified as High Risk and four as Moderate Risk.

Tianjin OEM Supplies had the highest procurement risk score of 84.46,
followed by Ningbo Distributor Supplies at 81.44 and Qingdao Distributor
Supplies at 81.39.

Long lead times, supplier reliability, cancellation rates and import
duty exposure were important procurement risk factors.

5. LEAD-TIME / DELIVERY RISK
Received purchase orders analysed:
21,630

Overall on-time delivery rate:
84.90%

Average delivery risk score:
42.75

Highest delivery risk score:
61.30

Lowest delivery risk score:
28.25

All 8 suppliers were classified as Moderate Delivery Risk.

Tianjin OEM Supplies had the highest delivery risk score, mainly
associated with its 28-day lead time and relatively low reliability
score.

6. SUPPLIER DIVERSIFICATION / SOURCING
The supplier base consists of:

OEM suppliers: 3
Distributor suppliers: 3
Local Vendors: 2

Regional distribution:

East China: 5
South China: 2
North China: 1

The overall sourcing structure was classified as Diversified.

However, East China represents the largest regional concentration,
so geographic diversification should continue to be monitored.

Average supplier performance score:
71.56

Highest performance score:
74.30

Lowest performance score:
68.85

Supplier performance classification:

Good Supplier Performance: 5
Moderate Supplier Performance: 3

Top performing suppliers:

1. Shenzhen OEM Supplies - 74.30
2. Guangzhou Local Vendor Supplies - 74.07
3. Suzhou Local Vendor Supplies - 73.48
4. Shanghai Distributor Supplies - 71.70
5. Hangzhou OEM Supplies - 71.45

The lower-performing suppliers were mainly affected by higher
procurement risk rather than poor procurement efficiency.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------
1. Maintain alternative sourcing options even though overall supplier
   concentration is currently diversified.

2. Prioritize risk mitigation for Tianjin OEM Supplies, Ningbo
   Distributor Supplies and Qingdao Distributor Supplies.

3. Monitor suppliers with long lead times, particularly Tianjin OEM
   Supplies and Hangzhou OEM Supplies.

4. Reduce purchase order cancellations for suppliers with comparatively
   lower procurement efficiency.

5. Review supplier pricing and negotiate better purchase costs where
   weighted cost variance is relatively high.

6. Continue monitoring East China sourcing concentration.

7. Use the supplier performance scorecard for periodic supplier review
   and procurement decision-making.

CONCLUSION
----------
Week 7 analysis provides a consolidated view of supplier cost,
procurement efficiency, dependency, risk, delivery performance and
sourcing diversification.

The supplier base is broadly diversified and procurement efficiency
is strong. However, procurement and delivery risks remain important,
particularly for suppliers with longer lead times, lower reliability
scores and higher cancellation exposure.

The supplier performance scorecard can be used as a management tool
to identify suppliers requiring closer monitoring, risk mitigation
or commercial improvement.
